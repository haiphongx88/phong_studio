// WHAT IS MICRO MOTION 3D?

SHORT-FORM 3D ASSETS BUILT FOR CONVERSION

Micro Motion assets are ultra-focused, 5-to-7 second CGI video loops designed to showcase complex product features. Built directly from your factory CAD data, these modular animations deliver instant visual impact across digital sales channels without the high cost of full video production.

01 // USE CASE

Shopify & Web Banners

Replace static photos on your product pages with smooth, silent 4K loops that show off materials, glass refractions, and LED components in motion.

02 // USE CASE

Social Performance Ads

Engineered in vertical (9:16) and square (1:1) formats to stop scrolling on Meta, Instagram, and LinkedIn ads in the first 2 seconds.

03 // USE CASE

Modular Pitch Decks

Insert lightweight WebM/MP4 loops directly into Keynote or web presentations for B2B buyer meetings and trade shows.
